A modern romance drama set in Shanghai.

Lin Yue, a talented but struggling fashion designer, receives a mysterious invitation to participate in an exclusive design competition hosted by the prestigious Shen Group. Upon arriving, she discovers that the competition's judge is none other than Shen Haoran, the cold and calculating CEO who once humiliated her designs years ago.

As the competition progresses, Lin Yue must navigate corporate intrigue, rival designers, and her growing attraction to the very man she swore to prove wrong. But Shen Haoran carries his own secrets — a family debt that threatens to destroy everything he's built, and a past connection to Lin Yue that neither of them remembers.

When the truth finally surfaces, they must choose between ambition and love, between the world's expectations and their own hearts.

Themes: love, redemption, ambition, class divide, identity
Visual style: cinematic, warm golden tones, modern Shanghai cityscape, fashion-forward
Target audience: Female audience (女频), young adults
